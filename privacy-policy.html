PAISA PAISA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PAISA PAISA is a personal finance application designed to help users organize and understand their financial information.

Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle account information such as your name, email address and profile details, along with financial information you enter, including income, expenses, transactions, budgets, savings goals, investments, loans, bills, assets and related notes.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide app features, maintain your account, synchronize your records, display financial summaries, and improve the reliability and security of the service.

Storage and Service Providers

Some account and financial information is stored using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Google Sign-In may be used to authenticate your account. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Information Stored on Your Device

App preferences, such as language, appearance and certain security settings, may be stored locally on your device.

Security

We use authentication and access controls intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and Deletion

Information is retained while needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations. To request assistance with deletion of your account or associated information, contact us using the email below. This contact route is a request channel; it does not mean that an automated in-app account deletion feature is currently available.

Children's Privacy

PAISA PAISA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law without appropriate consent.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
